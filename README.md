# LO: the first message on the internet

A 61-second narrated short that plays live in the browser.

29 October 1969, 22:30. Charley Kline at UCLA tries to log in to Bill Duvall's computer at SRI, 350 miles away, while
they talk on the phone. L. O. At G, the SRI computer crashes. So the first message on the internet was "lo", as in lo
and behold.

**Watch:** https://mys1erious.github.io/video-lo-1969/ (drag anywhere to scrub; "sound" for the narration).
The recorded video is in [`video/lo.mp4`](video/lo.mp4).

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole film (three.js scene, type, sound)
- `timeline.json`: when each word is spoken
- `vo/`: narration and the two phone voices
- `img/earth-night.jpg`: the planet at night

crash earlier.
